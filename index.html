About the reference data

0–5 years: WHO Child Growth Standards (2006). 5–19 years: WHO Growth Reference (2007). WHO publishes weight-for-age only up to age 10; after that, use BMI-for-age (needs height and weight from the same day). Heights under 2 years are compared as lying-down length, from 2 years as standing height; a 0.7 cm correction is applied when the measuring position doesn't match. Percentiles are for tracking, not diagnosis.
